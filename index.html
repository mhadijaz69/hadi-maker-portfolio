RESEARCH & PUBLICATION

Research behind the project

Published • 2026

Regression-Based Modeling of Flight Emissions and Per-Passenger Climate Impact

Sole Author • International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research (IJFMR), Volume 8, Issue 1

Through a ten-week research project, I investigated approaches to estimating aircraft fuel consumption and emissions using accessible aviation data and published methodologies.

The project required me to evaluate the limitations of available datasets, refine my original modelling approach, and develop a transparent framework for estimating total and per-passenger environmental impact. The work later became the foundation for my flight-emissions web application.
